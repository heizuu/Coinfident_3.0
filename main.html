The 50/30/20 rule

The 50/30/20 rule splits your money into three parts. It gives you a target to aim for without tracking every peso.

  • Needs (50%): boarding or rent, food, transportation, tuition, school supplies, and load or internet.
  • Wants (30%): milk tea, hangouts, subscriptions, games, and shopping.
  • Savings (20%): an emergency fund and money for goals such as a laptop or thesis.

Steps to build your budget

  1. Add up everything you receive in a month: allowance from family, scholarship, stipend, or part-time pay.
  2. Set your targets: 50% for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings. A ₱10,000 allowance becomes ₱5,000, ₱3,000, and ₱2,000.
  3. List your needs first, such as boarding, food, transportation, tuition, and load.
  4. Add your wants and keep them within 30% of your allowance.
  5. Set aside your savings as soon as your allowance arrives.
  6. Compare your plan with the targets and adjust whatever is over or under.

How your budget is rated

Each category is rated on its own. Your overall rating is the lowest of the three, and planning to spend more than your allowance is always Poor.

  • Good: needs at 50% or less, wants at 30% or less, and savings at 20% or more.
  • Medium: needs up to 60%, wants up to 40%, and savings at 10% or more.
  • Poor: anything beyond those limits.
