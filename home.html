A College Student’s Guide to Personal Budgeting

A beginner-friendly educational website that provides practical guidance on personal budgeting, expense management, and saving strategies. It aims to develop responsible financial habits through budgeting methods, practical examples, and easy-to-understand financial information.

What is Coinfident?

Coinfident is a website that helps college students take charge of their money. It explains how personal budgeting works, shows how to divide a monthly allowance, and points to trusted places to keep learning.

Many students receive a fixed allowance and run out before the month ends. Coinfident uses the 50/30/20 rule to make planning simpler: 50% of your allowance for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings.

Importance of Budget Management Among Students

Budget management is essential for students as it teaches financial responsibility, discipline, and wise decision-making. By properly allocating their allowance, students can prioritize their needs, control unnecessary spending, and set aside savings for emergencies. It also helps them become more independent and prepared to handle financial challenges, both during their academic journey and in the future.
