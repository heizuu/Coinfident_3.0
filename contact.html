About Coinfident

We are first-year Computer students at Batangas State University - The National Engineering University, Alangilan Campus. We built Coinfident to help students like us develop better financial literacy and budget management skills.

On this website you will find:

  • A short guide to the 50/30/20 rule for needs, wants, and savings.
  • A sample budget check that shows how an allowance is rated Good, Medium, or Poor.
  • Trusted articles and videos on budgeting and saving.
